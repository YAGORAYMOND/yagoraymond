BIO

Yago Raymond is an engineer and live coder who has practised live coding intermittently since 2018, working with SuperCollider, TidalCycles, Mercury, and Strudel. His practice tends toward ambient music and heavily processed sound, with a particular interest in real-time sound processing and transformation. He is currently developing N-ary, a live coding environment that emerged from the search for a tool better suited to his own way of making music.
